What Amy is designed to handle

Amy is framed as a general SDR, not a closer. Her job is to make early conversations more consistent, useful, and ready for the right human handoff.

✓
Consultative discoveryCaptures role, organization type, need, urgency, decision path, and next-step readiness in a conversational way.
✓
Routing intelligenceIdentifies when a prospect sounds like public sector, AI, cloud, security, solutions architecture, or account-exec follow-up.
✓
Sales-ready handoffCreates cleaner context for the team before a call: what the prospect needs, what they asked, and where the opportunity should go.

What to test

Try realistic prospect questions. The strongest signal is whether Amy gathers useful context without sounding like she is reading a form.
